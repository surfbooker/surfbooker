Wake up, check the surf, walk straight onto the beach.

Rwts sits on Broad Haven seafront, just metres from the water. It has a relaxed feel, with a strong focus on quality and local sourcing.

The rooms

With a location like this, requesting a sea view room is a no brainer. All rooms are beautifully decorated with hard wood furniture and the beds are particularly comfortable with a choice of pillows. Tea and coffee making facilities are included and the bathrooms are stocked with Organic New Dawn products.

Restaurant

The quality feel continues with a striking walnut dining table and bar top making a statement. Local artists and surfboard shapers have their work on display.

Food & Drink

Rwts serves seasonal food made with fresh, locally sourced ingredients wherever possible and it shows. Breakfast shouldn't be skipped and we're coming back to try the Honey Roast Fig Pizza.

The bar

There's a large selection of interesting local spirits on offer, including a Pembrokeshire rum which uses laver seaweed, also known as Welshman's caviar. The name derives from a famous Pembrokeshire pirate, 'Barti Ddu', or in English 'Black Bart'.

Front window nook

This seating area is open 24/7 to room guests. It's the perfect spot for working with a coffee or having a few drinks watching the waves roll in.

Surfing from here

Broad Haven is a long sandy bay facing west into St Brides Bay. It is a proper all-rounder: gentle summer waves for first-timers, and bigger, punchier surf when the swell builds through autumn and winter. Exposure means wave size and quality change with swell, wind and tide, so check the forecast and conditions on the day.

Explore Broad Haven

Useful additions to a surf-trip plan.

Little Haven

Walk south towards Little Haven by the coast. The beach route depends on the tide, so check tide times and use the coast path when the sand route is cut off.

Newgale and the Coast Path

Newgale is a more exposed beach to assess against the forecast. Broad Haven also sits directly on the Pembrokeshire Coast Path.

Days away from the beach

Options include seasonal Skomer trips, Sweet Home Alpaca, Picton Castle Gardens, St Davids and advance-booked pottery painting in Haverfordwest.

Broad Haven’s northern third has seasonal dog restrictions from 1 May to 30 September. Check current signs when you arrive.
